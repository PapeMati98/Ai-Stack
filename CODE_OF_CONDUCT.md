# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep
discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing other
people's private information are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), marking the report
as a conduct issue, or by contacting the team via [synaptivo.eu](https://synaptivo.eu).
Maintainers may remove content and restrict participation for anyone who
violates this code.
