# Revenue and Customer

Use this lens for pricing and packaging, sales motion, pipeline, account growth,
retention, customer journey, service design, and voice of customer.

## Commercial decision

Define the customer segment, job, buying trigger, alternative, value delivered,
buyer and user, sales motion, proof, and constraint. Separate customer evidence
from internal opinion.

## Pricing and packaging

- Choose a pricing metric that tracks customer value, remains understandable,
  and is difficult to game.
- Treat cost as a floor and evidence of value as the anchor.
- Make tiers correspond to distinguishable needs and a clear upgrade reason.
- Model contribution, payback, capacity, churn downside, migration, and customer
  communication before recommending a change.
- Change one material variable at a time when attribution matters.

## Pipeline and retention

Define stages by buyer evidence and exit criteria, not seller activity. Track
conversion, velocity, loss reason, concentration, expansion, retention cohort,
and forecast bias. Never infer intent from a CRM field alone.

## Customer journey

Map promise, entry point, critical task, moments of friction, support handoff,
value realization, renewal, and recovery. Link every proposed change to customer
evidence and an observable outcome. Use synthetic identities in test environments
and do not contact real customers without explicit approval.

Route experiments to `growth-experiments`, foundations to `marketing-context`,
and system changes to the existing CRM, data, and engineering specialists.
