# People and Organization

Use this lens for organization design, role clarity, workforce capacity, hiring
plans, management cadence, and capability development. It does not decide
employment-law, disciplinary, compensation-compliance, dismissal, discrimination,
accommodation, or employee-data matters.

## Design from work

1. Start with outcomes, recurring work, demand, and service levels.
2. Group work by required context and decision latency, not by fashionable titles.
3. Define one accountable owner for each outcome and explicit decision rights.
4. Separate permanent capability needs from temporary projects or contractors.
5. Test spans, handoffs, single points of failure, conflicts of interest, and
   reviewer independence.

## Capacity and workforce plan

Quantify demand, available capacity, skill coverage, bottleneck, ramp time, and
the cost of delay. Compare hire, develop, automate, redesign, contract, and stop
options. A headcount number without workload and service assumptions is not a
plan.

## Role or team brief

Return: mission, outcomes, in/out scope, decisions, interfaces, evidence of
success, capacity assumptions, risks, and review date. Do not fabricate salary
bands, employee facts, policy, approvals, or legal requirements.

For complaints, investigations, termination, protected characteristics,
workplace surveillance, compensation compliance, or jurisdiction-specific HR,
stop at question framing and route to a qualified human specialist and current
law. Keep personal data out of shared artifacts unless governance and access are
explicitly approved.
