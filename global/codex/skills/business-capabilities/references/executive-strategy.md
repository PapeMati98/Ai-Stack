# Executive Strategy

Use this lens for direction, portfolio choices, sequencing, resource allocation,
and executive decision records.

## Frame the decision

- Write the decision in one sentence and name the decision owner and deadline.
- Separate the desired outcome from the proposed solution.
- Identify the binding constraint: capital, capacity, time, capability, demand,
  regulation, dependency, or uncertainty.
- Define the baseline and the cost of delay or inaction.

## Build options

Use at least a baseline and one genuine alternative. Compare them on:

- strategic fit and measurable outcome;
- required capabilities, dependencies, and opportunity cost;
- reversible versus irreversible commitments;
- downside, leading indicators, and exit conditions;
- first evidence that can be obtained cheaply.

For uncertainty, use scenarios rather than a single precise forecast. Name the
assumption that separates each scenario and the trigger for changing course.

## Decision memo

Return: decision, context, evidence, options, recommendation, dissent or residual
risk, milestones, owner, and next review date. Escalate material conflicts of
architecture or strategy to `model-council`; compile approved delivery through
`plan-delivery` and `agent-graph`.

Do not turn a recommendation into permission to spend, contract, hire, publish,
deploy, or contact external parties.
