# Provenance and Adaptation

This skill was independently authored after a read-only audit of:

- source: `https://github.com/cbrock84/headcount`
- inspected commit: `1f3f550826c0dcabe7b70ad0ad76953d4ff22a98`
- source license at that commit: MIT
- inspection date: 2026-08-30

The source contained 16 department plugins and 143 prose skills, plus a useful
pattern for narrow role surfaces and independent review. Its department plugins
shipped skills but did not package the root-level department agent charters.
Technology overlapped heavily with this stack, broad marketing installation
would increase trigger noise, and legal, tax, HR, security, and finance material
could not be treated as current authority.

The local design therefore keeps only the architectural ideas that add value:

- one small router instead of 143 discoverable entries;
- decision lenses selected from the work, not simulated job titles;
- evidence, authority, handoff, and reviewer boundaries;
- focused finance, people, operations, revenue, customer, strategy, and content
  prompts loaded only when selected.

No source plugin was installed. No source script, agent charter, or executable
payload is included. Text was re-authored for this stack rather than copied
wholesale. The source repository's own clean-room and provenance claims were
self-attested by its checks and were not adopted as independent proof.
