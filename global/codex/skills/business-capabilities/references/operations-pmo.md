# Operations and PMO

Use this lens for repeatable delivery, cross-team programs, operating cadence,
service levels, process ownership, risk, and execution visibility.

## Operating model

- Define trigger, customer, outcome, owner, inputs, outputs, controls, and system
  of record.
- Map the normal path, exceptions, approvals, evidence, recovery, and escalation.
- Distinguish accountable owner, executor, consulted expert, independent reviewer,
  and approver. Avoid shared accountability without a final decision owner.
- Use a small set of outcome, flow, quality, and risk indicators. Every metric
  needs an owner, formula, source, cadence, and action threshold.

## Program control

Maintain a dependency-aware plan, decision log, risk/issue register, change
control, acceptance evidence, and stop rules. Report state by outcome and blocker,
not by activity volume. A green task list does not prove a working system.

## Cadence

Use the shortest cadence that can change a decision. Each meeting or review must
have an owner, pre-read, decisions, actions, and explicit cancellation condition.

For a formal SOP, runbook, RACI, or process map, hand off to
`operations-playbook`. For substantial implementation, compile dependencies with
`agent-graph` and use `accept-system` for acceptance. Do not execute production
operations or external communications from this lens.
