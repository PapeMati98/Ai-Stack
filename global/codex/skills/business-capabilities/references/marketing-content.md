# Marketing and Content

Use this lens for positioning, campaign architecture, editorial direction,
creative strategy, and briefs for image, video, voice, audio, or multimedia work.

## Foundation

Confirm that a reusable ICP, positioning, proof, and message map already exists.
If any of these foundations is missing or unverified, name the gap and stop this
lens; load `marketing-context` instead. When the foundation exists, preserve its
customer language separately from interpretation and new copy. Do not make
unsupported performance, legal, comparative, or product claims.

## Campaign or content brief

Include:

- objective, audience, stage, single action, and success metric;
- core message, proof, mandatory facts, prohibited claims, and brand constraints;
- channel, format, duration or dimensions, variants, accessibility, localization,
  and review owner;
- tracking and learning plan, budget or distribution assumptions, and stop rule.

For media, also specify scene or composition, product visibility, spoken or
on-screen text, voice, music, pacing, source assets, rights, and acceptance
criteria. Then route production to `video-gen`, `avatar-gen`, `voice-gen`,
`audio-bed-gen`, the available image capability, or the MiniMax route selected
by Agent Fleet.

Publishing, ad spend, outreach, customer data use, and account access require a
separate explicit approval and the relevant channel or privacy skill.
