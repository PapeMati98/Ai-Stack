# Review Checklists

## Factual

- Every claim maps to a current verified source.
- Fact, founder statement, assumption, projection and scenario remain labelled.
- Dates, customer references, traction, team and market claims are not inferred.
- Sensitive disclosure matches the owner decision.

## Financial

- Canonical values, formulas, units, periods, currencies and rounding reconcile.
- Historical actuals, management projections and scenarios are distinct.
- Ask, instrument, terms and use of funds match every artifact.
- No valuation or return assertion is presented as independent advice.

## Narrative

- The investment thesis is explicit and audience-specific.
- Problem, solution, evidence, business model, advantage, team and ask connect.
- Risks and unknowns are visible; generic filler does not replace evidence.
- The three formats adapt depth without changing facts.

## Visual

- Render every page or slide and inspect it, not only the source structure.
- Reject clipping, overlap, tiny type, weak contrast and inaccessible hierarchy.
- Charts disclose period, unit, source and uncertainty.
- Images do not simulate customer, product, team or operational proof.
- Visual boldness supports comprehension and is not spread across every page.

## Export

- Editable source and PDF both open and correspond.
- All pages render; fonts, images, links and charts survive export.
- One-pager PDF has exactly one page.
- PDF metadata and filenames are intentional and contain no internal secrets.

## Confidentiality

- No secret, credential, restricted personal data or unapproved investor detail.
- Every provider saw only an allowed, minimized evidence slice.
- Unapproved sensitive claims and internal review comments are absent.
- Distribution remains unauthorized.
