# Architecture decision contract

```text
problem/primary_metric/guardrails/min_effect: ...
data_contract: source, labels, groups/time, leakage controls, train-valid-OOS-final-holdout
hardware_budget: ...
baseline_ladder: rung 1..5 with result or NOT_RUN
hypotheses:
  - id: H1
    claim: ...
    falsifier: ...
    minimum_experiment: ...
    cost/risk: ...
    stop_rule: ...
recommendation: rung + model/strategy + evidence
promotion_condition: ...
unknowns: ...
```

The final holdout remains untouched until the locked comparison. A recommendation without a measured baseline or explicit `NOT_RUN` is incomplete. Explicit local Arbor campaigns can provide numeric evidence across ladder rungs after selection is frozen and a durable single-use final claim is created. Their records are not AG acceptance or an independent architecture review; see the conditional workflow in `new-training/references/arbor-campaign.md`.
