# Orchestration

## Recommended Roles

- evidence steward: source and claim provenance;
- financial maker: number ledger and scenarios;
- narrative maker: audience-specific structure and copy;
- IC critics: independent market, finance, product and risk perspectives;
- design maker: editable visual system and artifact layout;
- factual/financial reviewer: independent from content makers;
- visual/export reviewer: independent from design maker;
- parent integrator: resolves findings and preserves owner gates.

One reviewer may inspect several maker branches when the context fits, but a
maker cannot accept their own branch. Use cheaper capable models for early
checks and reserve expensive review for material conflicts or final acceptance.

## Bounded Loops

Use Agent Loop only where a criterion and stop condition exist. Default repair
budget is two rounds per rejected gate. A third round requires a new owner or
architecture decision, not a silent extension. Model failure or timeout is an
infrastructure result, not a substantive rejection of the artifact.

## Council Triggers

Use Rada Modeli when independent reviewers disagree materially about audience,
instrument, market framing, economics, risk disclosure or the financing thesis.
