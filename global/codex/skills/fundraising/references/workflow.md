# Workflow

## 1. Intake And Continuity

Resolve Knowledge Hub continuity before company research. A missing brief is a
discovery requirement, never proof that a company has no prior work. Record the
campaign objective, artifact set and owner decisions in `campaign.json`.

For an existing deck or memo, inventory both editable sources and PDFs. The
editable source remains authoritative for changes; the rendered PDF is the
evidence used by visual and export reviewers.

## 2. Evidence Freeze

Classify evidence before provider delegation. Minimize confidential material and
give each model only the redacted slice required for its role. Freeze source,
claim and number ledgers before narrative or design work. Changed evidence
creates a new binding and invalidates downstream receipts.

## 3. Parallel Work

Safe parallel branches after the freeze include:

- market/problem and product/solution narrative;
- business model and use-of-funds analysis;
- artifact-specific structure;
- investment-committee critique from independent lineages;
- visual direction against the same approved storyboard.

Do not parallelize competing edits to a canonical number or owner decision.

## 4. Repair Loop

Each rejection lists criterion, evidence and bounded repair task. Repair the
smallest responsible upstream node, refresh affected hashes and rerun every
invalidated downstream gate. Do not weaken criteria to obtain PASS. Escalate a
material business conflict to Mateusz or Rada Modeli.

## 5. Closeout

The suite stops at `READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW`. Final review covers the ask,
sensitive disclosures and intended audience. Sending and publication are a
separate authorized operation outside this skill.
