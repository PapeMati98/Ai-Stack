# Artifact Contracts

## Pitch Deck

Build a decision-focused sequence, not a fixed slide count. The minimum
questions are: why this problem, why now, why this solution, evidence of demand,
business model, market boundary, advantage, team evidence, financing ask and use
of funds. Omit an unsupported section or label it as open; never fill it with a
generic claim. Inspect every rendered slide for hierarchy, density, clipping,
contrast and numerical consistency.

## Investment Memorandum

Preserve an executive thesis plus source-linked sections covering company,
problem, solution, market, product, traction, economics, competition, team,
financing, use of funds, risks, assumptions and open diligence. Clearly separate
historical evidence from management projections and scenarios. Keep an editable
document and a reviewed PDF.

## One-Pager

The rendered PDF must contain exactly one page. Prioritize the investment thesis,
problem, solution, verified proof, business model, selected metrics, financing
ask and contact path. A one-pager is not a compressed deck: remove low-priority
content before shrinking type. Reject clipping, unreadable footnotes and claims
without traceable support.

## Cross-Artifact Rule

All artifacts share the same campaign, claim and number ledgers. Wording may be
adapted to format and audience, but facts, periods, units, currency, ask and
terms must not drift.
