# Marketing context contract

Return a Markdown or JSON decision record with these fields:

- `scope`: product, market, geography, date and excluded segments.
- `evidence`: source id, date, provenance, quoted customer language and confidence.
- `icp`: segment, trigger, buying role, constraints and disqualifiers.
- `jtbd`: situation, functional/social/emotional job, current alternative and outcome.
- `positioning`: audience, category, promise, differentiator and reasons to believe.
- `message_map`: problem, proof-backed message, objection, response and channel hypothesis.
- `gaps_and_tests`: unknown, impact, falsifiable test, owner and stop rule.
- `decision`: draft, validated, revise or blocked, with rationale.

Quality gates: distinguish fact, inference and hypothesis; date volatile claims;
do not invent market size, testimonials, personas, conversion claims or customer
quotes; flag missing consent or source access. The skill is read/draft only.
