# Voice Direction

Use this to prepare text before calling TTS.

## Script Prep

- Read the script aloud mentally before generation.
- Break long paragraphs into short speech-friendly sentences.
- Use commas and line breaks for gentle pauses.
- Use em dash sparingly; prefer periods and short clauses for stable TTS pacing.
- Spell unusual names phonetically only if first samples mispronounce them.
- Keep UI/product narration concrete: say what the user sees or does.

## Performance Presets

Calm tutorial:
- steady pace;
- low drama;
- clear nouns and verbs;
- no hype.

Product demo:
- confident, concise, slightly energetic;
- CTA at the end;
- avoid exaggerated enthusiasm.

Ad read:
- audience and emotion first;
- one core benefit;
- strong final line.

Character:
- define age range, texture, energy, scene context, and emotional beat;
- keep direction short enough that it does not pollute the spoken line.

Localization:
- preserve intent, not literal word order;
- verify names, numbers, dates, and currency.
